What you are comparing

The two days are not the same day. The trains are Wednesday 13 May 2026, from the official DELFI timetable. The flights are Wednesday 15 January 2020, from OpenSky radar records — there is no open flight schedule for any date, and the openly mirrored radar only covers early 2020. Both are ordinary mid-week days; neither is the other's date.

Trails are eight simulated minutes on both maps, so their lengths are directly comparable — the streak behind a jet is four to five times the streak behind an ICE because that is the speed difference. Same frame, same projection, same clock.

Trains follow the route geometry in the feed; flights are drawn great-circle-straight between airports, which is all the radar flight list carries. Rail counts scheduled long-distance and regional services and excludes urban transit; air counts every flight that touched a German airport, so a plane leaving the frame is still airborne and still counted.
